How does speaking work here?

Pick or upload audio in the panel on the right, which guesses male/female from the voice's pitch and applies that as the Gender filter above, narrowing this list to matching avatars. Click one here, then press Play in the right panel. Mouth movement is driven live from the audio's volume and frequency content (real viseme blend shapes, animated in your browser) - it approximates speech shapes rather than reading exact phonemes, which is what keeps this instant instead of another AI pipeline to wait on.
